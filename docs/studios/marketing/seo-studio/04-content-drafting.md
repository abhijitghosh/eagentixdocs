# Step 03: Research-Locked AI Content Drafting

The **Write** step transforms keyword opportunities into published, high-ranking articles without common AI pitfalls like generic filler, hallucinations, or unverified claims.

---

## 1. Seeding from Keywords

When you transfer a keyword from **Step 02: Keywords**, SEO Studio automatically seeds:
- **Primary Keyword & Variations**: Core search query and related LSI terms.
- **Search Intent**: Informational, commercial, or transactional intent.
- **SERP Competitor Structure**: Headings (H2/H3) and subtopics that Google currently ranks on Page 1.

---

## 2. The Research Pack vs. Completed Draft

Drafting in SEO Studio is a two-stage process:

```
[ Step 1: Research Pack ] ──▶ [ Review & Approve ] ──▶ [ Step 2: Full Draft ]
  - Outline & H2/H3 map          - Verify proof points        - Complete article
  - Target word count            - Check forbidden words      - E-E-A-T Author bio
  - Internal link suggestions    - Adjust tone                - Formatted markdown
```

::: warning Completion State Rule
The **Write** step is not marked <span class="status-done">Done</span> when only a research pack exists. It only transitions to **Done** once the full article draft has been generated and approved.
:::

---

## 3. Brand & Proof Locking

To ensure enterprise compliance and authenticity, the AI drafting agent is bound by constraints established in your [Site Brief](/studios/marketing/seo-studio/01-site-brief):

- **Proof Points**: The draft weaves in verified case studies, metrics, or certifications from your brief.
- **Voice Guidelines ("Say")**: Enforces brand messaging and approved nomenclature.
- **Forbidden Phrases ("Don't say")**: Explicitly prohibits competitor names, banned guarantees, or compliance redlines.
- **Publishing Metadata**: Appends the verified author byline, author credentials, and target call-to-action (CTA) URL.
