# Step 05: Link Architecture & Equity Gaps

Search engines navigate your domain using hyperlinks. The **Links** pillar evaluates both your internal link architecture and your external backlink profile.

---

## 1. Internal Link Equity & Orphan Pages

A common technical SEO problem is **orphan pages**—high-value content or product pages that have zero incoming internal links from the rest of your website.

### Internal Link Diagnostics
- **Orphan Page Detection**: Flags URLs indexed in sitemaps that have no internal inbound links.
- **Click Depth Distribution**: Measures how many clicks from the homepage are required to reach each critical page.
- **Anchor Text Diversity**: Analyzes whether internal anchor text uses descriptive, contextual keywords rather than generic terms like *"click here"*.

---

## 2. External Backlink Gap Analysis

Compare your domain's backlink profile against your primary competitors:
- **Referring Domains**: High-authority publications linking to competitors but not to your site.
- **Link Quality vs. Velocity**: Tracking new referring domain acquisition rates.
- **Content Link Magnets**: Identify which types of pages (e.g. original data reports, free calculators, industry glossaries) earn the most natural backlinks in your niche.
