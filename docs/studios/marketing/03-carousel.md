# Carousel Studio

Faceless niche slide-sets on one locked visual system — the whole post reads as one
brand, with no people and no likeness risk.

Not to be confused with Content Carousel: that one turns an article or post you paste
in into a swipeable explainer. This studio starts from a niche pack (food, skincare,
fitness, and similar worlds) and renders the slide-set for you. For a single polished
image, use Finished Ad Studio. For a video slideshow, use TikTok Slideshow.

## How it works

1. Pick a pack — one niche, one locked look.
2. Choose which slides to include — all on by default, toggle any off.
3. Add the hook — a relatable line overlaid on the first slide, auto-picked or your own.
4. Optionally animate the hook slide with a subtle loop.
5. Generate, download the slides, and copy the post text with hashtags.

## Packs and slides

Each pack is a complete world: its own visual system, its own slide list (tips, steps,
details, close-ups — whatever that niche runs on). Switching packs resets the slide
selection and the hook, because the new world has its own.

Slide chips show how many you have selected out of the pack's full set. Pick at least
one. Generating a subset costs less than the full set.

## The hook

The hook is a short relatable line shown on the first slide — the reason someone stops
swiping. It is overlaid on the slide, not baked into the image, so it stays crisp and
editable.

Leave the field blank and the studio picks one that fits the pack. Type your own to
override it. Turn the hook off entirely if the slides should speak for themselves.

## The loop (optional)

One toggle animates the hook slide with gentle ambient motion — the camera stays still
while the scene breathes. It renders as a short looping video alongside the slides.

It costs extra credits and extra time. Leave it off for a stills-only carousel.

## What you get

- A result line showing how many slides rendered out of how many requested, plus the
  hook used.
- A slide grid with every rendered slide, each downloadable on its own. Click any
  slide to view it large.
- Any slide that failed to render shows as failed instead of silently dropping — retry
  the pack or a smaller subset.
- The hook loop video with playback controls, when you asked for one.
- Publish-ready post copy: a per-platform caption plus hashtags written for the
  slide-set, ready to paste.
- Warnings in plain language when something needs your attention (for example a slide
  that needs a retry).

## Your past carousels

Carousels autosave to history. Reopening one restores the pack, the slide selection,
the hook, and the rendered slides and loop video exactly as they were.

## What uses credits

- Browsing packs, toggling slides, and typing a hook are free.
- Generating slides is the main cost — fewer slides cost less.
- The animated hook loop is an extra charge on top, in credits and in waiting time.
- Post copy and downloads are free.
