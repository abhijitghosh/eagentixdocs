# Content Waterfall

One pillar in, a month of assets out: paste a video transcript, newsletter, or
long post and get up to 60 native assets — posts, threads, carousels, shorts,
audiograms, articles, a lead magnet. Every brief carries a verbatim line from
your source (no invented claims) and names the studio that builds it, so nothing
on the list is a dead end.

The router, not the renderer: this studio plans the waterfall and hands each
asset to the studio that builds it best. For the building itself, see those
studios' guides.

## How it works

1. Bring the pillar — paste material or search a topic. Name the kind (transcript,
   podcast, newsletter, long post, talk, article) or leave it on Auto.
2. Set the scale — 10 to 60 assets — plus the goal (followers, email list, leads,
   sales, authority), the formats you want, platforms, destination link, brand,
   call-to-action, logo, music, and engines. Steering notes tune the angle.
3. Plan — the asset list with funnel stage, effort, and the builder studio per
   asset. Filter by format or funnel.
4. Generate assets one by one or as a batch with live progress, inspect each
   result, and schedule them on the calendar.
5. Ship the lead magnet as a PDF blueprint, and open any asset in its building
   studio for full control.

Starter presets (like a founder talk turned into 30 multi-channel assets) load a
complete example briefing.

## Formats it plans

LinkedIn posts · X threads · Carousel decks · Talking-head and faceless shorts ·
Audiograms · Quote and stat statics · Explainer diagrams · Comic strips · Motion
explainers · Newsletter editions · SEO posts · FAQ articles · Reddit posts ·
Seed comments · Polls · Lead magnets · Landing pages · Ad copy tests · TikTok
slideshows. Each tagged by platform, medium, effort, and funnel stage — filter
the plan to what this campaign needs.

## Handoffs that never dead-end

Every asset names its builder: carousel decks open in Content Carousel, quote
and stat cards in Finished Ad Studio, diagrams in Diagram Studio, comics in Comic
Strip, talking-head shorts in UGC Factory, motion explainers in Motion Graphics
Film, landing pages and lead magnets in Landing Page Studio, long-form in Content
Studio. Your inputs travel with the handoff, so the builder starts briefed.

## Calendar and ideas

Beyond the asset list: a calendar view that schedules the waterfall across the
month, and an ideas tab for angles that didn't make the plan. Inspect any asset
for its brief, generation, and status.

## What uses credits

- Brief, plan, filters, calendar, and handoffs are free or small.
- Planning the waterfall is a small amount; generating each asset is metered by
  its own kind (text light, video heavy).
- Batch generation runs the meter per asset with progress shown throughout.
- Lead-magnet PDF, downloads, and history are free.
