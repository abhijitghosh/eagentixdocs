# Reels Studio

The talking-head enhancer: upload a clip of a person speaking and get a finished
reel — the person cut out and composited over animated motion graphics, with
captions, music, an end card, and a cover thumbnail.

Sibling of Shorts Studio, different job. Shorts finishes a clip as-shot: captions,
grade, music, end card. Reels rebuilds the frame itself — the speaker cut out from
their room and set into moving graphics. If your clip needs no background magic,
Shorts is faster and cheaper.

## How it works

1. Upload a talking-head clip.
2. Pick the motion treatment — a ready-made FX pack, or build your own from
   subjects × motion × backdrop — previewed live before you render.
3. Add captions (bottom strip or giant behind-subject type), music, end card, and
   cover thumbnail.
4. Render, fix any misheard caption words, re-burn, and download with post copy.

## FX packs vs build mode

- **Packs** — ready-made treatments, each with a real rendered loop preview so you
  see the actual motion, not a description. New packs appear automatically.
- **Build** — compose your own: pick up to 6 subjects (what is shown), one motion
  (how it moves, or Auto to let the subjects vote), and a backdrop (or keep your
  own room). Add a headline of up to six words, an emoji, or country flags.

**Depth toggle** (off by default): with it off, effects play in front of you and
the reel lands fast. Turn it on and effects pass behind you too — which needs the
person cut out frame by frame, so it runs longer. Replacing the room with a
backdrop always needs the cutout.

Size, accent colour, and backdrop are pickers with live previews of your exact
combination over a stand-in figure.

## Captions with two placements

No subtitle files to hand-write — the audio is transcribed for you:

- **Bottom strip** — classic burned captions in your style and colour.
- **Giant behind-subject** — the podcast-short look: huge words set behind you.
- **Off** — no words on frame.

Spoken language is a picker, and a brand-words box fixes what transcription
mishears ("insight" → "inside") before it burns. After rendering, every caption
line is editable and re-burns onto the same cut — fixing a word never means
re-rendering the effects.

## Finish and cover

- **Music** — pick a mood, or leave it blank to keep the clip's own audio.
- **End card (optional)** — logo, closing line, and link as a scannable code.
- **Cover thumbnail (on by default)** — the front-of-video card with your headline,
  built to thumbnail rules so the reel reads in a grid.

## What you get

The finished reel plus a poster frame, the caption file, and any warnings — one
download button. Plus publish-ready post copy with hashtags written from the
clip's actual words.

## Your past reels

Reels autosave to history. Reopening one restores the clip reference, the FX pick
or build, captions and their edits, music, end card, and thumbnail — a re-export
with a different treatment takes seconds.

## What uses credits

- Uploading, browsing packs, live previews, and caption edits are free.
- Rendering the reel is the main cost — depth (cutout) and longer clips cost more.
- Caption re-burns are a small amount, not a re-render.
- Downloads, poster, caption file, and post copy are free.
