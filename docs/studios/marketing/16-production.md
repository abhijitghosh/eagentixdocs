# Production Studio

The casting foundry: generate the people and places your ads perform in.
Characters become the reusable presenters behind UGC and Motion ads; locations
become the scene references those ads render in. Cast once, perform everywhere.

This studio makes cast, not ads. For the ads themselves, use UGC Ad Factory or
Motion Ad Studio — both pull their faces and sets from here.

## Two asset types, two ways to direct

- **Characters** — presenters, founders, customers, crowds.
- **Locations** — the rooms, streets, and sets scenes happen in.

And two directing modes:

- **Presets** — chips for region, gender, age band, framing, pose, wardrobe,
  cultural tribe and context, and style. New options appear automatically as they
  ship; you never type what you can pick.
- **Describe** — freeform direction in your own words, with an optional refinement
  line to steer the take.

Set how many to generate per combination and let the grid fill as renders land,
one by one.

## Casting with intent

- **Regions and age bands** default to a working set (East, Southeast, and South
  Asia; young adult to adult) — widen or narrow to the audience.
- **Skin-tone casting** — market casting for the tones a market expects, or
  representative range for deliberate breadth.
- **Poses** stay front-facing-safe: only framings that survive the talking-head
  lock are offered, so a presenter you cast today still performs tomorrow.
- **Wardrobe** suggests itself from your picks, or pin a preset. Cultural tribes
  and contexts tune the look without caricature.

## Quality gates

- **Diversity QA** runs on every batch by default: the set is checked for
  uniqueness, so a "group of five" never comes back as the same face five times.
- **Identity QA** checks every restyle against its source face — restyling is where
  faces drift, so the verdict lands on the result before you approve it.
- **Skin enhance** is an optional paid super-resolution pass per avatar, for
  close-up work.

## Library, views, restyle

- **Library** — every saved character and location, searchable across all metadata
  with filters for region, background, gender, age, and framing. Delete with
  confirm.
- **Views** — additional angles of a saved character for flexible staging.
- **Restyle** — new wardrobe or look on the same identity, verified by the
  identity check.

Characters saved here appear as the cast pool in UGC and Motion Ad Studios, with
the identity lock that keeps each face identical across every beat and re-render.

## What uses credits

- Browsing presets, the library, and views are free.
- Generating characters and locations is the main cost — more combinations cost more.
- Diversity and identity checks ride with the generation; the skin-enhance pass is
  one extra paid call per avatar.
- Restyles are metered per result; downloads are free.
