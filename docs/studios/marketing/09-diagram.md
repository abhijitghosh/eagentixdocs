# Diagram Studio

Describe a process in words — get a clean labelled diagram. For how-it-works
sections, explainer decks, reports, motion-graphics beats, and enterprise visual
suites.

## How it works

1. Describe the process step by step, and name what the diagram is for.
2. Pick a style, a size, your brand colour, and an optional logo.
3. Generate — the studio plans the figure, renders it, and checks the rendered
   image against your text (mislabelled steps, garbled text, missing pieces) with
   automatic refine passes.
4. Edit the description and re-render, or download every format.

A 3-beat enterprise preset (Bottleneck → Engine → Outcome) loads a complete example
you can replace with your own words.

## Styles and engines

- **Style gallery** — soft tech, flat vector, blueprint, whiteboard, isometric,
  minimal mono, and more, each with a best-practice hint. Your brand accent shapes
  the look.
- **Render choice** — Auto, Vector (exact labelled boxes and arrows with validated
  geometry, plus an interactive version), or Image (illustrated render).
- **Diagram type** — leave it to the planner, or pin architecture, workflow,
  sequence, data flow, or lifecycle when you know what you need.
- **Review passes** — how many refine rounds the checker may run before showing you
  the result.

## Sizes

16:9 Deck · 1:1 Social · 4:3 Report · 9:16 Story. A full-suite run renders the same
diagram tuned for every size at once.

## What you get

- The diagram image, clickable to view large.
- A vision-check trail: what the reviewer verified on the render, shown with the result.
- **SVG** — high-precision vector for print and docs.
- **PNG** — ready-to-post image.
- **Interactive HTML** — self-contained explorable version (vector engine).
- **Presentation slide** — branded slide download for decks.
- **All formats in this suite** — one click when you rendered the full set.
- Publish-ready post copy for the diagram.

## Your past diagrams

Diagrams autosave to history. Reopening one restores the words, style, brand
colour, logo, and rendered result with its review trail.

## What uses credits

- Describing the process and picking styles are free.
- Generating the diagram is the main cost — refine passes and full-suite
  multi-size runs cost more.
- Downloads and post copy are free.
