# Animation Studio

Your real images, animated: drop photos in, pick a motion template with a live
layout preview, add a backing track, and get a finished video. No brief, no
waiting on AI to draw — nothing is generated, everything moves.

For AI-made video, use UGC Ad Factory, Motion Ad Studio, or Template Ads. For
slideshows from text, use TikTok Slideshow. This studio is the motion layer over
pictures you already own.

## How it works

1. Drop your images — each template takes a set number; extras beyond the max are
   ignored with a note.
2. Pick a motion template — every one shows a live layout preview, so you see the
   choreography before you render.
3. Write the copy the template asks for — each template labels its own fields
   (one line per product with prices, quote plus reviewer, stat plus label).
4. Set format, accent, duration (3–15 seconds), music, and the end card.
5. Render, download the MP4 and poster, and copy the post text.

## Templates you can pick

Hero reveal · Carousel · 3D flip · Kinetic type · Before/after · Stat counter ·
Product showcase · Testimonial / review proof · Catalog drop · Device mockup ·
Feature callouts · Photo dump · Fancy slideshow · and more — 20 in all, each with
its own copy contract so prices, quotes, and stats land pixel-perfect.

The fancy slideshow adds GPU shader transitions between images, including a mix
that plays them all.

## Finishing touches

- **Subject fill** (on by default) — auto-crops each image to its subject so
  faces and products never get awkwardly cut.
- **Sizes** — square, vertical, wide, portrait.
- **Music** — a backing track by mood, or silent.
- **End card** — the standard logo, closing line, scannable link, and address card.

## Your past animations

Work autosaves to history. Reopening restores images, template, copy, format,
music, and end card — a re-render in a new size takes seconds.

## What uses credits

- Uploading images, browsing templates, and writing copy are free.
- Rendering the video is the metered step — longer durations cost more.
- Poster, downloads, and post copy are free.
