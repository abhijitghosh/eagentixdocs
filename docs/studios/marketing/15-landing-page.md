# Landing Page Studio

Goal plus offer in, live-quality page out: the place your ads point at. Pick what
the page must do — capture leads, sell, book calls, build a waitlist, fill an
event — and the planner assembles the right sections with benefit-led copy,
rendered as one self-contained responsive page in your brand.

For a full multi-page site, use Website Studio. This studio is one page with one
job.

## How it works

1. Pick the campaign goal and give the offer — product, price or promise, and the
   facts behind it.
2. Style it — brand colour and voice, market (so generated imagery shows the right
   faces and architecture), plus an optional inspiration screenshot to borrow the
   look. Design DNA only: layout ideas transfer, nothing is copied.
3. Add proof — client logos, gallery, testimonials, stats. Proof renders only from
   real material you supply; nothing is invented.
4. Build the lead form from the field registry and choose lead quality (volume,
   balanced, or qualified). Add your analytics ID.
5. Generate, preview at every screen size, check the ad→page message match, then
   publish to a live link or download the page.

## Goals you can pick

Lead capture (form → your CRM) · Direct sale (product + offer) · Booking / call
(book a slot) · Waitlist (early access) · Event (register / RSVP). The goal
decides the sections, the form, and the closing — a waitlist page and a sale page
are different machines.

## Proof, hero, and motion

- **Proof drawer** — logos as image links, gallery shots, testimonials as
  quote | name | role lines, stats as value | label lines. Empty means the section
  ships without proof rather than with invented proof.
- **Hero image** — upload your real photo, generate one, or enhance yours. It can
  also be animated into a looping cinemagraph hero.
- **Quality panel** — responsive previews plus image and copy direction passes
  before you publish.

## Message match

The silent killer of paid performance is an ad that promises one thing and a page
that says another. Upload the ad creative and the studio scores headline-plus-offer
match between ad and page, with what to fix. Run it before you spend.

## Publish and campaigns

Publish to a live link under your chosen slug, with unpublish when the campaign
ends. Fork the page per campaign so each ad set gets its own variant, and
download the self-contained HTML any time — no framework, no bundler, works
anywhere.

## Your past pages

Pages autosave to history with campaigns attached. Reopening restores the goal,
offer, brand, proof, form, hero, and the published state.
