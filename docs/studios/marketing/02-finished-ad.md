# Finished Ad Studio

One designed, on-brand static ad — real headline, bullets, and call-to-action laid over
the scene, ready to post.

Use this when you need a single polished image. For a talking presenter on video, use
UGC Ad Factory. For multi-scene video, use Motion Ad Studio. For swipeable multi-page
sets, use Carousel Studio. For fast templated variants, use Template Ads.

## How it works

1. Describe the product — paste a product page link or type the details and photos.
2. Confirm your brand — colours, logo, and look carry over automatically.
3. Draft the copy — a headline, supporting lines, proof, and call-to-action, all editable.
4. Pick a layout and sizes — the layout decides where copy sits; sizes decide where it can run.
5. Generate, then score and improve — check the creative score, fix the weakest part, and
   download what passes.

## Layouts you can pick

| Layout | Best for |
|---|---|
| Copy left / Copy right | Classic product-plus-message split |
| Solid panel | Busy photos that need a clean reading area |
| Bold claim | One big promise as the hero |
| Proof / stats | Ratings, numbers, and credibility |
| Us vs Them | Comparison against the alternative |
| Before / After | Visible transformation |
| Callouts | Pointing at details on the product or scene |
| Review | A customer quote as the lead |

The picker shows a live mock in your brand colours, so you are choosing the layout,
not guessing from a name.

## Sizes and where they run

| Size | Where it runs |
|---|---|
| Square 1:1 | Feed |
| Portrait 4:5 | Feed (taller, more screen) |
| Story 9:16 | Story, Reel, TikTok |
| Wide | LinkedIn, Google |

Pick the platforms you are targeting and the studio sizes the ad for each. Previews
show the ad in a real feed frame, and downloads are plain PNGs ready to post anywhere.

## Product photos that help

Three optional slots beyond the main shot:

- **Alternate view** — the opposite side or back.
- **Label detail** — packaging and small print, so text renders correctly.
- **Scale / in use** — the product at real size and in context.

The more of these you give, the less the design has to guess.

## Copy you control

Everything is editable before anything renders: headline, bullets, badges, rating,
and the call-to-action. An offer check scores the draft out of 100 — below 70 means
strengthen it first, with concrete suggestions shown inline.

## Score and improve

- **Creative score** — every generated ad can be scored out of 10, with its weakest
  dimension named and fixes listed.
- **Improve** — one click renders stronger variants that fix the weakest part, so you
  can compare before and after.
- **Quality gate** — ads with a critical defect (unreadable text, broken layout, failed
  checks) stay blocked from export until fixed or regenerated. Warnings are advisory.

You can also download an animated HTML5 banner version for ad slots that accept it.

## Your past ads

Ads are saved to history automatically. Reopen any past ad, resume its product, copy,
and layout, and generate a fresh version without retyping.

## What uses credits

- Browsing layouts, editing copy, and opening history are free.
- Drafting copy uses a small amount.
- Generating the ad images is the main cost.
- Scoring and improved variants each use a small additional amount.
