# Announcement Studio

News, not ads. A launch, a funding round, a partnership, or a pricing change becomes
a branded social card, a clean press cut a journalist can actually use, and the
written pieces — every figure traced back to your source facts.

## How it works

1. Pick the kind of news — or leave it on Auto and paste your release, email, or
   notes. The studio works out the kind and pulls the source facts out of your own
   words, without rewriting them.
2. Fill the source-facts box — round size, dates, names, and the approved verbatim
   quote with who said it. Nothing reaches a card that isn't in here; a number with
   no fact behind it is rejected, not softened.
3. Add brand name and logo, an embargo line if the news is still under hold, an end
   card (one line plus link), and optional music.
4. Write the announcement, edit the copy spine, check the news gate, then render.
5. Download the cuts and write the press pieces from the same facts.

Each kind of news shows what it cannot ship without (the facts that kind requires),
so you know what you still owe it before you render.

## The news gate

The gate is the product here. It reads first, before any render button:

- **Blocked** — the copy is not shippable as news yet. Fix what it names.
- **Warning** — it ships, but a journalist will ask the question. Shown, never hidden.
- **Clean** — nothing flagged.

It also names any facts you still owe the kind. Written pieces carry their own gate
verdict with them.

## The copy spine (editable before you render)

Announcement copy is signed off by a person, never tuned by a test — so everything
is editable: headline (one line break per display line, up to 3), standfirst, quote
with name, title, and organisation, date stamp, source line, and ticker items (one
fact per line; empty means no ticker scene). The kicker pill (BREAKING, EMBARGOED,
and the like) is empty by default — add one only when the headline cannot carry it.

Quote edits are checked against your source facts: the quote must stay verbatim and
attributed.

## The two cuts

Rendered side by side from the same copy, same brand bar, same end card:

- **Social cut (9:16)** — the branded card for feeds and stories.
- **Press cut (16:9)** — the clean landscape cut for press kits, partners, and the
  newsroom package. Branded, not de-branded.

Filenames carry the headline, so a folder of press assets stays sortable. An embargo
line watermarks every frame until the hold lifts.

## Written pieces

The same facts also produce the surfaces video cannot reach — press release and
similar formats, each with its purpose described before you write it. Each piece
opens in a drawer with copy-to-clipboard and its own gate verdict attached.

## Your past announcements

Announcements autosave to history under the headline. Reopening one restores the
kind, the news, the facts, brand and embargo, the copy spine, the written pieces,
and both rendered cuts — finished work is never lost and never needs paying for twice.

## What uses credits

- Picking the kind, pasting the news, and filling facts are free.
- Writing the announcement is a small amount.
- Rendering each cut is the main cost.
- Each written piece is a small additional amount.
- Downloads and post copy are free.
