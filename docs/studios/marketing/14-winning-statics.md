# Winning Statics Studio

Two ways to a proven static: swipe the structure of a winning ad with your own
product and copy, or elevate your existing ad to top craft. Both end at the same
export gate — critical defects block the download.

For original designed statics from scratch, use Finished Ad Studio. For fast
templated batches, use Template Ads. This studio starts from a reference: a
competitor's winner, or your own ad that should perform better.

## The two modes

- **Swipe a winner** — upload a competitor's static plus your product. The studio
  reads the reference into a fill-in template (its layout plus every detected text
  slot), you replace the copy and logo, and it renders your version faithful to
  the original's composition — one render or several variants.
- **Elevate my ad** — upload your existing ad. It gets graded with craft
  prescriptions (what to fix and how), rebuilt, and re-graded so you see
  before-versus-after proof.

## Swipe: how it works

1. Upload the winning ad and your product photo, plus the product name.
2. Analyze the reference — its layout and detected text become editable slots with
   hints per slot.
3. Fill the slots with your copy and your logo. Leave the scene alone unless you
   name an explicit visual change — blank means the analysed scene is frozen, so
   the swipe stays faithful instead of drifting into a new ad.
4. Render one or several faithful variants.
5. Clear the export gate and download, with post copy grounded in the ad's own text.

## Elevate: how it works

1. Upload the ad to upgrade, your product, and the approved product facts — the
   only claims the strengthened copy may use.
2. Get the grade with prescriptions and copy changes.
3. Download the elevated rebuild with its before/after verdict.

## The export gate

Every render is scored before it can ship. One critical defect — unreadable text,
broken layout, failed checks — blocks export; polished misinformation is worse
than no ad. Warnings allow a download-with-warning. The gate runs on every image
in a batch, not just the first.

## Your past statics

Work autosaves to history. Reopening restores the mode, uploads, product, slots
with your copy, analysis, renders, and elevation results.

## What uses credits

- Uploading, analyzing the reference, and editing slots are free or small.
- Rendering variants is the main cost — more variants cost more.
- Elevate's grade plus rebuild is metered as one job.
- Downloads and post copy are free.
