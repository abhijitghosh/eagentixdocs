# Shorts Studio

The finisher for footage you already have: upload a clip and get a post-ready
vertical short — word-synced captions burned in, a music bed underneath, an
optional colour grade, and a branded end card.

This studio generates nothing from scratch. For AI-made video, use UGC Ad Factory
(single presenter), Motion Ad Studio (multi-scene), or Template Ads (motion
graphics). For slideshow-style shorts from stills and text, use TikTok Slideshow.

## How it works

1. Upload a clip — MP4 up to 200MB. Vertical 9:16 works best, and it needs audible
   speech for captions.
2. Style the captions, pick the language, grade the look, and choose music.
3. Add the optional end card — logo, closing line, link, address.
4. Hit Make short — transcription, caption burn, music mix, and end card take a
   couple of minutes.
5. Download the finished short plus post copy with hashtags.

## Captions

On by default, styled from a visual gallery — tap a tile to preview the look on
your own words. Pick the colour too, including a custom brand colour. Spoken
language covers auto-detect plus English, Spanish, Thai, Indonesian, Vietnamese,
Hindi, and Mandarin. Turn captions off entirely for a clean clip with just music
and end card.

## The look and sound

- **Colour grade** — a gallery of grades previewed on your own footage, with a
  strength slider. Applied to the source clip only, so captions and the end card
  keep their colours. Off by default, and ungraded is the right default for most
  clips.
- **Music bed** — pick a mood; the mix lays it under the speech.

## End card (optional)

Logo, one closing line, a link rendered as a scannable code, and an optional
address line — the same branded card every other studio ships. Leave it empty and
the short ends on your footage.

## What you get

The finished short in a phone-frame preview with badges showing the caption style,
grade, music, and end card — one download button. Plus publish-ready post copy
written from the clip's actual spoken words.

## Your past shorts

Shorts autosave to history. Reopening one restores the clip reference and every
setting, so a re-export with a different caption style or grade takes seconds.
