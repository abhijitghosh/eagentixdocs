# TikTok Slideshow

Swipeable photo slideshows for TikTok — paste a topic or a few lines, get portrait
slides with a caption, a seed comment, and an optional narrated video.

Cousin of Content Carousel, tuned for TikTok instead of LinkedIn: vertical 9:16,
punchier decks, art styles that fit faceless accounts, and TikTok-native caption plus
first comment. For LinkedIn document posts, use Content Carousel. For theme-pack
niche slide-sets with no writing, use Carousel Studio.

## How it works

1. Type or paste — a topic, a few lines, or a longer piece. Search mode can start
   from a topic alone.
2. Pick the deck shape, goal, and audience warmth — or leave everything on Auto.
3. Choose images or text-only, an art style, a visual concept, and your palette.
4. Generate the slides, refine any slide's wording, and preview the swipe order.
5. Export PNGs, a PDF, or a narrated MP4 — plus caption, comment, and post copy.

## Deck shapes you can pick

Auto (pain funnel), or: Pain funnel (5–7) · Tips (3–5) · Value (6–8) · Before/after (3) ·
Feature → benefit (6–10) · Story (10–12).

Goals: saves, comments, shares, followers, or DMs. Audience: cold reach or warm leads.
Both steer the framing and the close.

## Images or text-only

- **Generate images** — full-bleed visuals rendered for every slide in your chosen
  art style.
- **Text only** — skip image rendering for a fast typographic deck.

Art styles: Photo (clean lifestyle/product default) · Cartoon (bold 2D, faceless-account
style) · Anime · Illustrated (hand-drawn editorial sketch) · Bold graphic (flat poster,
2–3 colours) · 3D render (clay objects on pastel).

You can also upload up to 4 of your own images — screenshots or product photos — and
add your palette via a brand kit or a visual concept. A force-fresh-media toggle
re-renders everything instead of reusing approved visuals.

## Refining

Each slide's text is editable, with a per-slide refine box: describe the change and
only that slide updates. Concept variants show up to 3 alternate visual directions
for the same words — pick the one you like and continue with it.

## What you get

- Portrait slides with prev/next navigation plus a view-all grid. Click any slide to
  view it large.
- Per-slide PNG downloads, a multi-page PDF, and an MP4 video — the deck as a paced
  slideshow with slide-left motion, optional progress bar, backing track, and optional
  per-slide voiceover you can edit line by line.
- A TikTok caption (refine it inline), a seed first comment to post under the video,
  and publish-ready post copy with hashtags.
- Notes on audio fit and Reels crossover when they apply.

## Your past slideshows

Slideshows autosave to history. Reopening one restores the topic, deck shape, art
style, concept, palette, uploads, slide edits, voiceover lines, caption, comment,
and rendered slides and video.

## What uses credits

- Typing, picking shapes and styles, and editing slides are free.
- Rendering slides is the main cost — text-only is cheapest, fewer slides cost less.
- Narrated MP4 exports cost extra, in credits and in waiting time.
- Caption, comment, post copy, and downloads are free.
