# Competitor Swipe & Adapt

Find what competitors post, learn why their winners win, and adapt the patterns
to your product in your voice. Steal the structure, never the content: hooks,
formats, and angles transfer — their footage, copy, and brand do not.

The one intelligence tile inside the Produce tier, deliberately: intelligence
tiles prevent churn, and this one belongs where most customers will actually see
it. Deeper always-on monitoring lives in Competitor Intelligence.

## How it works

1. Name the competitors — a brand name or website finds their profiles for you,
   or add handles by hand. Instagram and TikTok scan directly; other platforms
   still show the brand's footprint so you know where they play.
2. Set the window (7, 14, 30, or 90 days) and how many top reels to pull, plus
   what you sell and your brand voice — both sharpen everything downstream.
3. Scan, read the strategic brief (what they're focused on, not a list), then
   tear down individual winners.
4. Adapt the teardown to your product — the hook, structure, and angle rewritten
   for what you sell, in your voice.

If a brand has no profile on the platform you picked, the studio says where they
actually are instead of scanning nothing.

## The reel grid

Every top post shows its thumbnail, creator, caption, and views, likes, and
comments — the evidence behind the ranking. Open the original any time; the grid
is for deciding what deserves a teardown.

## The teardown

Per reel, on demand: the hook with its type called out, the structure that holds
the post together, and the adaptation — the same idea rebuilt for your product
and voice. Teardowns are the deliverable; everything else is how you choose
which ones earn it.

## What uses credits

- Discovering profiles, scanning reels, and reading the brief are small.
- Each teardown is metered on its own — tear down the winners, not the grid.
- Adapted copy rides with the teardown; history and links are free.
