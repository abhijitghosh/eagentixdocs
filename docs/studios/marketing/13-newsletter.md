# Newsletter Studio

A full issue in one place: pitch the topic, get a structured edition with subject
line and body, dress it with a hero and motion visuals, check it against quality
gates — then send it to your list, publish it to WordPress, and post it to
LinkedIn without leaving the studio.

## How it works

1. Brief it — paste material, search a topic, or type a seed. Add who the reader
   is, what you are against, your brand, call-to-action, and footer boilerplate.
2. Pick the issue type — or leave it on Auto and the brief decides.
3. Generate the issue, edit anything, add hero and motion visuals.
4. Clear the quality gates, then ship: email campaign, WordPress article, LinkedIn
   post — each from the same issue.

## Issue types you can pick

Auto-select, or: Point of view (thesis → proof) · Playbook (steps → checklist) ·
Case study (problem → result) · Benchmark (finding → meaning) · Digest
(updates → synthesis) · Comparison (criteria → trade-offs) · Announcement
(change → action).

Short issues under 600 words are fine for announcements; anything else that thin
gets flagged.

## The issue

Every issue ships with a subject line, inbox preview text, title, designed email
body, plain-text body, markdown, a 155-character WordPress excerpt, and LinkedIn
post text. Three body views: rendered preview, markdown, and HTML. Copy any of
them — HTML for designers, plain text for Gmail-style sends.

## Visuals

- **Hero image** — generated for the issue, or uploaded. Keep-hero means re-renders
  never redraw approved art; regenerate only what you ask to change, with a style
  medium picker (auto, diagram, cartoon, isometric, photo).
- **Motion callouts** — stat counters, bar charts, and pull quotes rendered as
  motion assets or stills, placed where you choose (intro, sections, bottom).
- **Narrated carousel explainer** — the issue retold as a voiced slide video with
  per-slide narration you can edit, pronunciation rules for tricky terms, and MP4
  or GIF embeds dropped into the body.

## Quality gates

Checks run before anything ships: excerpt length, placeholder links, proof-needed
markers, thin body. Blockers stop the send; the rest is advisory. Figures posted
to LinkedIn must already live in a published article — exact numbers ship with a
citation, never bare.

## Ship it three ways

- **Email** — pick the list, sender name and address, and send now or schedule for
  later. Review links can reopen the exact session.
- **WordPress** — draft or publish with category, author, and excerpt. Republishing
  updates the same post in place. The link feeds back into the LinkedIn post.
- **LinkedIn** — post text with optional media, posted to your account in one click.

## Your past issues

Issues autosave to history. Reopening one restores the brief, type, brand, body
with edits, hero, motion assets, and every publish result with its link.
