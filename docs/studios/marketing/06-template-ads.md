# Template Ads

Fast, editable motion-graphic video ads from proven scene types — price drops,
countdowns, offers, catalogs, testimonials, lookbooks. Prices and dates render
pixel-perfect because every scene is code, not an AI-painted picture.

Three modes in one studio:

- **Templates** — fastest and most reliable. The studio plans your ad from a library
  of proven scene types and you edit every scene's text, colours, and photos before
  rendering. Best for promos, offers, events, and product ads.
- **Freeform** — describe any motion-graphic idea in plain words (explainers,
  simulated demos, animated charts, launch teasers). Maximum freedom, less
  predictable, takes longer.
- **Audiogram** — give it a topic and it writes a short two-voice podcast-style
  conversation, narrates it with studio voices, and renders a waveform video. Great
  for thought-leadership clips and teasers.

## How it works

1. Describe the ad in a sentence or two — or start from a starter (price drop, offer
   stack, testimonial, education explainer, lookbook).
2. Add the product: page link, photos, name, price, and call-to-action. Photos handed
   over from the product studio carry across automatically.
3. Bind your brand kit (or let the studio derive colours from your logo or page) and
   pick music.
4. Review the scene plan — every scene editable in plain words — then render.
5. Download the videos, poster frames, stills, and the campaign pack.

## What you control per scene

Each scene shows its purpose in human terms — headline, offer line, urgency line,
quote, brand name, button text — with colour swatches instead of code. Fix any scene
by typing what you want ("make the price bigger", "use the white sneaker photo").
Added photos are guaranteed to appear: leftovers get their own scene before the end
card rather than being dropped. Off-brand or wrong-product photos are flagged and
removed with a note telling you what went.

The closing scene always carries your link as a scannable code.

## Formats and outputs

- Renders every placement size you asked for from the same scenes, reflowed per
  orientation so portrait, square, and wide all look intentional.
- **Campaign pack** — every placement video plus poster frames and the editable plan,
  in one click.
- Still PNG exports from the same plan, alongside video.
- Localize the whole ad's copy into another language without rebuilding the scenes.

## Audiogram mode specifics

Pick waveform or video look, bars/line/dots waveform visuals, solo or duo speaker
layout with karaoke highlighting, narration voices for host and guest, and language.
The script is written from your topic plus the offer and proof your product carries.

## Your past ads

Ads autosave to history under the product or brief name. Reopening one restores the
brief, brand, photos, music, scene plan with your edits, and rendered outputs.

## What uses credits

- Typing a brief, editing scenes, and browsing history are free.
- Planning the scenes uses a small amount.
- Rendering video is the main cost — more placements and longer durations cost more.
- Audiogram narration and localization each add a small amount.
- Downloads and the campaign pack are free.
