# Content Carousel

Paste a blog post, release notes, or any article — get a swipeable, on-brand
LinkedIn / Instagram carousel. Every slide is code-rendered (legible headlines,
stats, comparisons, diagrams, a CTA close), grounded in your source, with your logo
and palette. No AI-photo text gibberish.

For a faceless niche slide-set that starts from a theme pack instead of your words,
use Carousel Studio. For a video slideshow with music, use TikTok Slideshow.

## How it works

1. Paste your content (or just a topic) and pick the deck type and goal.
2. Pick a look — your brand kit or a curated feed theme.
3. Generate the plan, edit any slide's text, then render the slides.
4. Export as images, a LinkedIn document PDF, or a narrated video — plus post copy.

## Deck types you can pick

Auto, or one of: 3D Mascot Story · Tips · Value · Guide · Curation · Story · Quotes ·
Before/after · Problem-agitate-solve · Data story · A vs B · Myth vs fact ·
Feature → benefit · Testimonial · Founder POV.

Each type has its natural length (tips run 3–5 slides, guides 8–12, and so on). Slide
counts offered are 5, 6, 7, 8, or 10. Tell it your goal too — followers, saves,
comments, shares, email list, sales, or DMs — and whether this is cold reach or warm
leads, so the framing and close match.

## The look

- **Brand kit** — your locked palette plus logo. When a kit is active the slides use
  it throughout; clear the kit to browse curated themes instead.
- **Curated themes** — gallery tiles that show exactly how slides look in that theme,
  so you pick with your eyes.
- **3D mascot mode** — describe the mascot character once (or upload a reference image
  as its identity anchor) and the whole deck stars the same character.

## Editing before you render

The plan comes back as editable slides: reorder, rewrite any headline or point, and
regenerate the narration line for a single slide without touching the rest. Applying a
different theme or concept restyles the deck without rewriting your words. Everything
stays grounded in the source you pasted — claims come from it, not from invention.

## What you get

- Slide images, one per page, downloadable individually.
- **PDF (LinkedIn)** — one multi-page file, the document-post upload format; the file
  name comes from your article's opening words.
- **MP4 / GIF video** — the same deck as a swipeable video, paced per slide with a
  slide-left motion, optional progress bar, backing track, and an optional narrated
  voiceover that adds sourced context beyond the on-slide copy.
- Publish-ready post copy that ships with the carousel.
- Sizes: 4:5 portrait for LinkedIn, 1:1 square.

## Your past carousels

Carousels autosave to history under a name taken from your topic. Reopening one
restores the content, deck type, theme, mascot, layout edits, narration lines, and
rendered slides and video.

## What uses credits

- Pasting content, picking a deck type, theme, or brand kit are free.
- Planning and rendering the slides is the main cost.
- Narrated video / GIF exports cost extra, in credits and in waiting time.
- Downloads and post copy are free.
