# Connecting Integrations

Eagentix connects to external search engines, crawling services, and AI foundation models to perform factual analysis.

---

## 1. Google Search Console (GSC)

Connecting your Google Search Console account gives Eagentix direct access to your verified search performance, impressions, click-through rates (CTR), and crawl issues.

### How to Connect
1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Integrations** in the top navigation or left sidebar.
2. Click **Connect Google Search Console**.
3. Log in with your Google account and grant read-only Search Console permissions.
4. Once authenticated, your verified properties will appear in tool dropdowns across SEO Studio.

::: tip Multi-Property Accounts
If your Google account owns multiple domain or URL-prefix properties, you can assign different GSC properties to distinct client sites in the Studio.
:::

---

## 2. DataForSEO

DataForSEO powers high-throughput SERP rank checks, search volumes, keyword difficulty scoring, and competitive backlink crawls.

- **Market Accuracy**: Supports localized search results down to city-level and country-specific Google engines (e.g. `google.com.sg`, `google.co.uk`).
- **Real Organic Metrics**: Distinguishes organic SEO difficulty (0–100) from Google Ads PPC competition.
- **SERP Features**: Detects AI Overviews, Featured Snippets, Local 3-Packs, and Video carousels.

---

## 3. Large Language Model (LLM) Providers

Eagentix leverages state-of-the-art LLMs (OpenAI GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Google Gemini 1.5 Pro) for content drafting, brief synthesis, and SERP intent analysis.

All model prompts are strictly locked to your verified site brief to prevent hallucinations.
