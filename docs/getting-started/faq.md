# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is an Eagentix Studio?

A Studio is a guided workflow for one job — video ads, carousels, newsletters,
landing pages, SEO. You approve the plan before anything renders, and everything
stays grounded in facts you supplied. See the
[Marketing Studios overview](/studios/marketing/00-overview) for the full map.

## Do I need to write prompts?

No. Every studio uses pickers, briefs, and editable drafts — formats, styles,
voices, and layouts are choices, not prompt engineering.

## What uses credits?

Planning and rendering. Browsing, picking, editing, history, downloads, and post
copy are free. Each studio guide ends with a "What uses credits" section listing
exactly what is metered.

## Will the AI invent claims about my product?

No. Copy comes only from facts you supplied — product details, proof points,
allowed claims. Anything off-limits is never used or inflated, and proof sections
render only from real material.

## What happens to my past work?

Every studio autosaves to history. Reopening restores inputs, edits, and renders
exactly as they were — finished work is never lost and never needs paying for
twice.

## Which studio should I use?

Start at the [Marketing Studios overview](/studios/marketing/00-overview). Rule of
thumb: quick talking-head video goes to
[UGC Ad Factory](/studios/marketing/01-ugc-factory), multi-scene storyboarded video
to [Motion Ad Studio](/studios/marketing/10-motion-ad), LinkedIn carousels to
[Content Carousel](/studios/marketing/04-content-carousel), TikTok slides to
[TikTok Slideshow](/studios/marketing/05-tiktok-slideshow), news to
[Announcement Studio](/studios/marketing/07-announcement), rankings and audits to
[SEO Studio](/studios/marketing/seo-studio/00-overview).

## Where do I start with SEO?

The [SEO Studio guide](/studios/marketing/seo-studio/00-overview) runs six pillars
in order: site brief, keywords, tracking, drafting, on-page, links, and monitoring.
Begin with the [Platform Overview](/getting-started/overview), then the site brief.
